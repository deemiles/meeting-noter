What it does

Built for people who
don't want to upload their meetings

Every other tool starts by asking for your audio. This one runs the whole pipeline on your machine.

Speaker labels, no diarization

Your mic and the system audio are captured as two separate tracks, transcribed independently, then merged by timecode. Every line comes out tagged Me or Them.

Records the call, not your desktop

ScreenCaptureKit filters by application, so it captures Slack or your Google Meet tab — and leaves the rest of your screen out of the video entirely.

Nothing leaves the machine

whisper.cpp runs locally — large-v3-turbo does an hour of audio in a couple of minutes on Apple Silicon. Recordings live in a plain folder you own.

Summaries on demand

Press ✨ and the transcript goes to the Claude CLI, which writes back topics, decisions, and action items as summary.md. The only step that touches a network.

Searchable history

Full-text search across every transcript you have recorded, straight from the menu bar. Plus .srt subtitles for every call.

Updates itself

Sparkle checks for new versions in the background and installs them in place — signed with a stable key, so your permissions survive every update.

Getting started

Four minutes to your first transcript

Two of those minutes are downloading the Whisper model.

1

Install Whisper and a model

Transcription runs through whisper.cpp. Any .bin model works — the app picks the largest one in the folder.

brew install whisper-cpp

mkdir -p ~/Library/Application\ Support/MeetingNoter/models
curl -L -o ~/Library/Application\ Support/MeetingNoter/models/ggml-large-v3-turbo.bin \
  https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp/resolve/main/ggml-large-v3-turbo.bin
2

Download and drag to Applications

Open the .dmg and drop Meeting Noter into your Applications folder like any other app.

3

First launch: right-click → Open

Meeting Noter is signed with a self-signed certificate rather than a paid Apple Developer ID, so macOS will say it "cannot verify the developer". Right-click the app icon and choose Open, then confirm once. Every later launch — and every auto-update — works normally.

Prefer not to trust a stranger's binary? Build it yourself in one command:

git clone https://github.com/deemiles/meeting-noter.git
cd meeting-noter && ./scripts/build-app.sh
4

Grant screen and microphone access

Hit record once and macOS will ask for Screen & System Audio Recording plus Microphone. Restart the app after granting the screen permission — a running process does not pick it up. Then ⌘⇧R starts and stops a recording from anywhere.
